# QA Report — V2.5.5.36

- Titan Shield: corrected conditional effects. Perfect Dodge +20 is no longer unconditional; refine +9 and base-stat gates are required. Refine +12 grants MHP/MSP +10% and +5% resistance to supported elements.
- World Light Asprika: added +30% resistance against supported elemental attack types and +30% physical damage against small/medium/large targets.
- Known incomplete effects: Instant Teleport Lv.1, post-cast delay -10%, and magic damage against all sizes are not implemented because the corresponding active-skill / combat-delay / magic-size consumers are not yet present. These are intentionally not represented as working bonuses.
- Regression suite: 20/20 passed.
